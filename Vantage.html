Vantage

Personal academic tracker

Role

Product & Content designer

Timeline

4 days

Tools

Figma, Figma Make, Claude

Deliverable

High-fidelity prototype

Who

Graduate students moving through a multi-year program.

What

One app for program progress, coursework, and reading.

Why

Most school tools only look ahead. Vantage shows students how far they’ve come. Students can also look back to search for specific assignments, readings, or contacts made during a program.

Intro

Students seldom look back to see how far they’ve come.

Learning is often treated as a one-time endeavor: enroll, do coursework, graduate, forget. Students seldom look back to see how far they’ve come.

Calendar apps, LMS platforms, and note-taking apps are forward-facing. They help students complete the next task. But learning happens best when there’s integration and context.

Vantage was designed to provide both. It serves as a cockpit during the program and a vault of knowledge after graduation.

Challenge

Designing a single app to integrate program dashboard, progress tracker, and task management functionalities. Vantage was designed to stand out as a comprehensive application that helps students stay oriented and keep moving forward.

Results

  • A complete end-to-end prototype covering onboarding through all core features
  • Every data point in the app is derived, not manually entered
  • The information architecture scales from one course to a full multi-year program
  • The visual language remains consistent across 25+ screens

Design decisions

Course details

The course detail view was designed around the mental model of physical folders to keep course-specific details separate.

Assignments, readings, and notes live within a single page so students never lose context switching between them.

Course details

Overview

The Overview consolidates program progress, upcoming deadlines, and course navigation into a single glanceable screen so orientation happens in one tap.

Staying motivated requires seeing the big picture and the immediate next step at the same time.

Overview

Library

Graduate students consume information from prescribed textbooks, independent reading, and course-specific assignments.

The library is designed to help students visualize their growing list of current readings as well as books they wish to read next.

Reading progress is tracked visually, giving students a sense of accomplishment with small wins.

Library

Reflections & next steps

Vantage is an app I see myself using as a graduate student currently saving assignments and prescribed readings in separate folders on my laptop. When an idea comes from a genuine need, it begins to take shape almost on its own.

Developing Vantage pushed me to think about the problem from multiple perspectives, as a UX designer, a developer, and a brand designer. This shift helped me make more grounded and intentional decisions throughout the process.

Given more time, I would focus on conducting user research to understand whether this is a need shared by others, and how the app could better support them. From there, I would aim to take Vantage beyond a high-fidelity prototype and move it into development.
