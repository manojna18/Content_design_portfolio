About

I use language, visual and verbal, to solve problems.

My writing practice has crossed industries: journalism, marketing, technical writing, and now product design.

Each one taught me something different about how people receive information, and what it costs them when it isn't clear.

I think in systems, but I start from feeling. My best work begins with something I've lived and asks whether it's true for someone else too. Usually it is.

That instinct runs through everything I make.

When I'm not designing, I'm experimenting with a new painting medium, trying something new in the kitchen, or negotiating the survival of three potted plants. I live in Detroit with my husband and, optimistically, all three of them.
